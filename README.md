# HR_Analytics
Human Resources (HR) Analytics is the process of using the data and improve how organization manages its employees. This data can include about the employee performance, attrition rate, active employees, etc. The goal is to make informed decision for workforce productivity and HR align strategies and improve decision making for business objectives.
This project analyzes employee attrition and workforce trends using Microsoft Excel on a dataset of approximately 50,000 record. The goal is to provide actionable insights for HR and leadership teams to reduce turnover, enhance retention strategies, and support diversity and workforce planning. By leveraging Excel’s advanced features like Pivot Tables, Slicers, KPIs, and Conditional Formatting, this project delivers an interactive dashboard that transforms raw data into strategic business intelligence. This project is ideal for demonstrating data analytics skills in HR/People Analytics, particularly for roles requiring Excel proficiency and business problem-solving.


The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of employee demographics, attrition patterns, and departmental trends across a workforce of 50,000 employees. It’s designed to help HR leaders identify retention risks, optimize workforce planning, and improve employee experience.

🔹 Top-Level KPIs
- Employee Count: 50K
- Active Employees: 24,895
- Attrition Rate: >50.21%
- Average Age: 39
- Male Research Scientist Rate: ₹114.45
These headline metrics offer a snapshot of workforce size, engagement, and compensation benchmarks.

🔹 Departmental & Role Insights
- Highest Attrition Rates: Support (55%) & Software (50%)
- Average Working Years:
- Software: ~6 years
- Research & Development: ~1 year
- Work-Life Balance Ratings: Range from 2.41 to 2.47 across roles
This section highlights where retention is weakest and where employee experience may need improvement.

🔹 Demographic Trends
- Attrition by Age Band: Highest in 18–24 group (51.12%)
- Attrition by Marital Status: Fairly even across Married, Divorced, Single (~33%)
- Attrition by Promotion History: No promotion in last 0–4 years linked to higher attrition
These insights help HR teams target retention strategies by age, life stage, and career progression.

🔹 Compensation & Income Analysis
- Monthly Income Bands:
- Low: 6,311 employees
- High: 30,079 employees
- Attrition vs Income: Lower income bands show higher attrition risk
This suggests a potential link between compensation and employee turnover.

🔹 Filters & Interactivity
- Gender and Department filters allow dynamic slicing of all visuals
- Enables targeted analysis for specific groups or business units

🎯 Business Impact
This dashboard empowers HR leaders to:
- Identify high-risk departments and roles
- Understand attrition drivers by age, income, and promotion history
- Prioritize retention efforts for younger employees and under-promoted staff
- Benchmark compensation and work-life balance across roles
